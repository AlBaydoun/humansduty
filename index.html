Our story

A duty carried since 2004

EST 27.09.2011 · N° 137 · BINT JBEIL, LEBANON

Human's Duty is a Lebanese NGO and non-profit, founded on 27 September 2011 under Interior Ministry notification no. 137. Its roots reach back to the Lebanese Sports Club of Bint Jbeil, established in 2004.

In 2006 we opened a center teaching computers and English. Over two consecutive years, with OTI support funded by USAID, nearly one hundred young women and men trained in youth, environmental, health, social and sports awareness. Freed from dependency and sectarian intolerance, they became the conscious, independent generation of the Bint Jbeil region.

From those seeds grew the Lebanese Vocational School of Life, and in 2011, Human's Duty. Our work continues across Lebanon: Bint Jbeil, Beirut, Mount Lebanon, Koura and beyond.
