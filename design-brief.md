# Human's Duty — design brief (Phase 0, locked)

## Design read
Lebanese NGO (Bint Jbeil, since 2004 roots) speaking to diaspora donors and the local community. Register: dignity, resilience, sacred duty. Emotional arc across the page: grief > defiance > hope.

## Concept spine
**"The Descent of the Phoenix"** (journey/waypoints). The visitor descends WITH the phoenix from the burning night sky down to the ground of Bint Jbeil, then walks the story forward into dawn. Scroll = altitude + time. Night is literal (war, ignorance); dawn is literal (education, relief, rebuilding). The page background is ONE continuous journey gradient: ash night > ember > cedar dusk > dawn ivory > logo teal on light.

## Delivery tier
**spectacle** (client asked: 3D where it fits, wild visuals, cursor-reactive everything).

## Locked palette (client-brand override of dark+ember ban: the client's phoenix artwork IS fire on night, and client chose the "ashes to dawn" journey)
- Ash night `#0B0908`, elevated `#1A1412`
- Fire accent (one evolving accent): vermilion `#E2483D` > molten gold `#F2A33C`
- Cedar `#2E6B4F`, deep cedar `#14342B`
- Dawn ivory `#F4EDE2`, warm sand `#E7D9C2`
- Logo teal `#1B7FA6` / `#2AA187` (final chapter, links on light)
- Within any single viewport: one dominant accent. Defense: the accent's hue shift IS the narrative.

## Locked type
- Latin display: **Cabinet Grotesk** (self-hosted, Fontshare). Latin body: **Inter Tight**. Data/mono: **JetBrains Mono** (bank table, meta).
- Arabic display: **Alexandria**. Arabic body: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**. Full RTL mirror.
- No serif anywhere.

## Tier-1 technique (wow-catalog)
- **A2 long-form chaptered scrub**: the phoenix descent film (2 chained clips, same grade: sky descent > swoop over town as embers turn to dawn light) scrubbed across a ~400vh pin. Chapter headlines swap per beat; right-edge progress rail reads as an ALTITUDE METER. Defense: the spine is a descent; scrolling literally flies the phoenix down.
- **Second beat (different family): D1 horizontal cinema rail** for the story timeline 2004>2026; history travels sideways like a road. Oversized numeral "2004" as background structure (= the single second-read moment).
- **Seasoning: B2 grade-shift cursor spotlight** in the 2024 memorial: the cursor is a candle revealing the destroyed center in the dark.
- **Custom cursor** (spectacle contract): small ember dot + trailing glow; hue follows the journey (ember > gold > teal). Touch: none. Reduced motion: static everything, composed final states.
- Ambient ember/dust particle field, cursor-reactive drift. Never behind-text-only decoration; it reacts.

## Mobile degradation (declared)
Scrub pin shortens to ~220vh with coarse frames; cursor effects off (scroll drives spotlight + parallax); rail becomes native swipe with snap; bento 2-col then 1-col; masonry 2-col.

## Copy rules honored
No em/en dashes anywhere. Headlines <= 8 words. EN corrected slogan: "Together, we make a difference." AR: "معاً نصنع الفرق". One contact label. No scroll cues, no fake stats; real org facts only (est. 27/09/2011, notification 137, ~100 youth trained 2006-2008, destroyed 2024).
